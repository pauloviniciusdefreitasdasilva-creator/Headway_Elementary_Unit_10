NOTE ON TRANSCRIPTS
The lesson uses the supplied scripts to align question prompts and teacher keys. It does not reproduce full book/video scripts as one long transcript. The statistics in “What’s happening in the world right now?” and times in the video are treated as details stated in the course recording, not as current factual data.
